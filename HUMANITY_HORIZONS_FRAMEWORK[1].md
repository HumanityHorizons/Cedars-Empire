# Humanity Horizons Institutional Readiness & Impact Framework (HH-IRIF)

**Owner:** Humanity Horizons  
**Product:** Humanity Horizons 360  
**Positioning:** Independently maintained internal framework for institutional readiness, responsible programme delivery, funding application preparation and evidence-based impact management.

## Framework pillars
1. Institutional governance, eligibility and accountability.
2. Programme logic, needs analysis and evidence.
3. Results-based planning and measurable outcomes.
4. Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning.
5. Responsible budgeting, internal controls and financial records.
6. Procurement integrity and conflict-of-interest management.
7. Safeguarding, prevention of exploitation and abuse, and safe complaints pathways.
8. Fraud prevention, reporting and investigation procedures.
9. Data protection, confidentiality, information security and records retention.
10. Inclusion, gender equality, disability access and equitable participation.
11. Environmental and social risk assessment and mitigation.
12. Partner capacity, subgrant oversight and proportionate assurance.
13. Stakeholder communications, feedback, response and remedy.
14. Sustainability, exit planning and responsible handover.
15. Workforce capabilities, ethical technology use and lifelong learning.

## Readiness evidence sequence
For each requirement, record: requirement; relevance; risk level; evidence expected; evidence location; evidence owner; status; gap; corrective action; due date; reviewer; and last-verified date. Suggested statuses are **Not assessed**, **Gap identified**, **In progress**, **Evidence ready**, **Reviewed**, and **Not applicable with rationale**. A ticked control means the user attests that evidence exists; it does not independently validate the evidence.

## Workforce readiness areas
Candidates can self-assess: digital/AI literacy; data analysis; cybersecurity hygiene; analytical and systems thinking; creativity; communication; collaboration; adaptability; lifelong learning; project delivery; finance/budgeting; monitoring and evaluation; stakeholder engagement; leadership; inclusion; safeguarding; ethical judgement; and environmental stewardship. Each score should be supported by candidate evidence or a learning plan. The system must not infer or fabricate credentials.

## Governance and maintenance
- Framework owner: Humanity Horizons.
- Review frequency: at least annually and after a material incident, regulation change or platform capability change.
- Each revision records version, date, editor, reason for change and approving Humanity Horizons role.
- Use a requirement-by-requirement checklist for each actual opportunity or agreement.
- Clearly distinguish self-attestation, documentary review and independent verification.
- Do not publish claims of universal compliance, external certification, guaranteed award success or guaranteed employment.
- Imported listings retain accurate issuer and original-source details when needed to prevent misleading users; the Humanity Horizons framework remains a separate proprietary product.
