# Humanity Horizons 360 — proprietary framework, privacy and operational notes

## Ownership and positioning
Humanity Horizons independently owns and maintains the Humanity Horizons 360 product identity, the **Humanity Horizons Institutional Readiness & Impact Framework (HH-IRIF)** and the **Humanity Horizons Future Workforce Readiness Matrix**. The platform's public interface uses Humanity Horizons branding. Do not place outside institution logos or wording that implies approval, partnership, certification or endorsement without a separately authorized basis. The platform does not seek external approval to own or publish its own framework.

Imported opportunities, advertised vacancies, grant calls and public notices must still preserve accurate publisher names, source links, eligibility rules, deadlines and attribution when needed to identify the original listing. Such source details are part of the factual content of a listing, not a claim of ownership or affiliation by Humanity Horizons.

## Humanity Horizons Institutional Readiness & Impact Framework (HH-IRIF)
The framework organizes self-assessment across the following control areas:

1. Eligibility, governance and institutional due diligence.
2. Theory of change, outputs, outcomes, indicators, baselines, targets and evidence.
3. Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning; data quality and disaggregation.
4. Financial planning, authorization, segregation of duties, reconciliations, audit trail and asset control.
5. Procurement, documented competition where applicable, supplier due diligence and conflict-of-interest controls.
6. Safeguarding, codes of conduct, prevention of exploitation and abuse, safe reporting and confidential referrals.
7. Fraud prevention, anti-bribery, whistleblowing, investigation and protection against retaliation.
8. Privacy, lawful basis, consent where applicable, minimization, access controls, retention and incident response.
9. Gender equality, disability inclusion, accessibility, non-discrimination and safe participation.
10. Environmental and social risk screening, mitigation and do-no-harm practice.
11. Stakeholder participation, complaints, feedback, response times, closure and learning.
12. Partner/subgrant capacity, roles, agreements, monitoring and proportionate assurance.
13. Records, version control, responsible communications and evidence provenance.
14. Exit, handover, ownership, maintenance and sustainability.

This framework is a practical readiness aid. It is not a universal compliance guarantee, legal opinion or substitute for the exact criteria in an opportunity, agreement, jurisdiction or project risk profile. A completed self-assessment is not independent verification. Humanity Horizons should review and version the framework periodically, document changes, and assign accountable reviewers.

## Humanity Horizons Future Workforce Readiness Matrix
The CV/resume studio encourages candidates to evidence current capabilities and identify development needs across:
- AI and data literacy, responsible use of digital tools, cybersecurity and technological literacy.
- Analytical and systems thinking, evidence use, problem solving, creativity and communication.
- Adaptability, resilience, curiosity and lifelong learning.
- Project and operations management, budgeting, monitoring, evaluation and data visualization.
- Leadership, collaboration, stakeholder engagement and talent development.
- Environmental stewardship, safeguarding, ethical practice, inclusion and accountability.

These are development prompts, not a prediction of future vacancies or a guarantee of employment. Never invent a candidate's qualifications, skills, job history or achievements; candidates must verify all claims.

## Quality assurance and claims
- Use Humanity Horizons naming consistently in page titles, exported templates, interface labels and framework documents.
- Do not use third-party logos or imply that a named institution has approved or certified this platform.
- When analysing a live opportunity, preserve the issuer's real instructions and use those as the decision criteria for that submission.
- Mark missing facts, unsupported claims and estimates clearly; keep original source files and a record of key assumptions where lawful and appropriate.
- Include human technical, safeguarding, finance and management review before high-stakes submission or implementation.
- Review procurement, due diligence, sanctions screening, safeguarding, environmental/social safeguards, reporting formats and cost rules only to the extent required by the actual opportunity and applicable law.

## Privacy by design in this prototype
- Anonymous sessions use a random session identifier and timestamp rather than collecting IP addresses or device fingerprints.
- Identifiable attendance/follow-up is stored only for signed-in users who opted in.
- Upload/download operations request a user-stated B2B/workspace purpose and rights confirmation. Declared purposes are recorded in an operational audit trail.
- Authentication and admin tools require additional production hardening. Do not rely on this prototype for sensitive beneficiary records or confidential safeguarding case files.
- Generative AI is optional and requires explicit user opt-in at the relevant screen. Review the configured service's terms, data handling, residency and cost before enabling it. Do not send confidential or sensitive personal data without authorization and a reviewed lawful basis.
- Establish a retention schedule, user access/correction/deletion/export workflow, backup and restore testing, account recovery, incident response and role-based access before production.
- Check applicable data-protection, cyber-security, employment, safeguarding, financial and recordkeeping laws with qualified advisers in each country served.

## Treasury safety
The treasury area is an admin-only ledger and reference display. It does not connect to TRON, custody keys, verify chain receipts or initiate withdrawals. Never store a seed phrase or private key in code, a repository, application secrets or the database. Live payments require a separate security design with key custody, address/network validation, transaction simulation, limits, multi-person approval, monitoring, reconciliation, fraud controls and applicable legal/tax/accounting review. Any future revenue model must be plainly described to users; downloads do not automatically create revenue.
